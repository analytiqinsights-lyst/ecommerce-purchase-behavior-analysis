# E-Commerce Purchase Behavior Analysis
End-to-end e-commerce data analysis using SQL, Excel, and Tableau to uncover revenue, customer, and product insights.
Tools: SQL | Excel | Tableau

🔍 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce purchase behavior to identify key revenue drivers, customer segments, product performance, and seasonal trends. The objective is to deliver business-ready insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

🎯 Business Objectives
Identify top revenue-generating products and categories
Analyze customer purchase behavior and segmentation
Understand regional and seasonal sales trends
Support data-driven decision-making for growth and retention
Data Cleaning & Preparation
Removed duplicates and invalid records using SQL
Standardized date formats and categorical fields
Created calculated fields for KPIs such as AOV and repeat purchase rate
Validated data consistency before visualization

📈 Dashboard Highlights (Tableau)
KPI Overview: Revenue, Orders, Customers, AOV
Revenue Trends over Time
Product Category Performance
Customer Segmentation (High / Medium / Low Value)
Geographic Sales Analysis
Interactive filters and drill-downs

💡 Key Business Insights
A small number of product categories contribute the majority of revenue
High-value customers account for a disproportionate share of total sales
Seasonal spikes suggest opportunities for targeted campaigns
Certain regions show high order volume but low AOV, indicating upsell potential

🚀 Outcome
This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow—from raw data to executive-level insights—suitable for business intelligence, consulting, and freelance analytics engagements.
